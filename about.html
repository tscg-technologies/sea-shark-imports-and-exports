Corporate Profile

Who We Are.

SEA SHARK IMPORT AND EXPORT PRIVATE LIMITED operates in the import and export business, focusing on sourcing and supplying different products between India and other countries according to business requirements and product availability.

We aim to facilitate business opportunities through product sourcing, supply coordination and international trade activities. Our corporate culture values clarity, reliability and close coordination with both suppliers and commercial buyers.

Scope of Operations

Our Business Activities.

We channel our operational capabilities across three interconnected pillars of cross-border commerce.

Product Sourcing

We identify, evaluate and coordinate with authentic producers and suppliers to secure quality agricultural commodities and commercial goods that align directly with specific buyer requirements and seasonality.

Import & Export Activities

Executing cross-border commercial transactions, bridging Indian export opportunities with global business markets and examining inbound international products required for the domestic market.

Air Cargo Transportation

Arranging prompt cargo transportation via airline cargo networks to preserve product integrity, uphold delivery timelines and provide dependable international transport coordination.

Execution Methodology

Our Business Approach.

At SEA SHARK, our approach to international commerce is grounded in diligence and structured execution across every phase of trade:

  • Understanding business requirements: We engage closely with clients to define product specifications, volume needs and timing expectations.
  • Identifying suitable product sourcing opportunities: Evaluating authentic sourcing channels based on seasonal availability and market conditions.
  • Coordinating import/export activities: Ensuring procedural alignment, trade document preparation and smooth customs coordination.
  • Supporting shipment arrangements through air cargo: Organizing airline transport space to facilitate rapid transit and maintain product freshness.
