Multimodal Transit Solutions

Air Cargo & Ocean Shipping.

SEA SHARK IMPORT AND EXPORT PRIVATE LIMITED coordinates international shipments through both air cargo and ocean freight shipping lines.

Our logistics solutions adapt to product characteristics, order volumes, urgency, and destination specifications:

  • Air Cargo Transport: Ideal for perishable goods such as fresh grapes, chillies, and urgent consignments where speed is vital to preserving freshness and commercial quality.
  • Ocean Shipping & Container Freight: Tailored for high-volume exports, bulk commodities, and heavy cargo requiring cost-effective full container load (FCL) or less-than-container load (LCL) seafreight handling.

From port drayage to customs clearance and terminal handoffs, we ensure seamless shipment coordination throughout the supply chain.

Core Logistics Focus

Freight Forwarding & Transit Areas.

We structure our trade logistics activities across three key operational coordination areas.

1. Airline Cargo Transportation

Booking scheduled commercial airline cargo capacity for rapid transit of perishable fresh produce and time-sensitive cargo across international air routes.

2. Ocean Shipping & Seafreight

Arranging full container load (FCL) and consolidated shipping vessel routes for bulk export commodities, heavy goods, and large volume trade consignments.

3. End-to-End Customs & Dispatch Support

Assisting commercial partners with standard trade documentation, port & airport handoffs, customs clearance support, and transit milestone tracking.

Operational Flow

Shipment Coordination Process.

A structured four-step procedure designed to provide clarity from enquiry through flight departure.

01

Step 1: Business Enquiry

Client initiates consultation detailing product category, volume expectations and target country destination.

02

Step 2: Product & Shipment Requirements

Confirming specifications, seasonal availability, packaging requirements and agreed commercial parameters.

03

Step 3: Shipment Coordination

Scheduling packaging, staging goods for airport handling and preparing compliant international trade paperwork.

04

Step 4: Air & Ocean Freight Dispatch

Dispatch via scheduled commercial flights or designated ocean container lines to destination terminals.

*Note: The actual process and transit timelines depend specifically upon the individual shipment, customs clearances, airline schedule availability and mutual business arrangements.
